I’m MJ—an AI product manager who builds.#

I think deeply about how AI products actually work: why LLMs hallucinate, how context engineering shapes behavior, and what separates successful AI adoption from expensive experiments.

This blog is where I work through ideas in public—grounded in real experience shipping AI products and helping teams navigate the messy reality of enterprise AI adoption.

What I write about#

  • Context engineering — how the context you provide shapes LLM behavior
  • AI product management — the craft of shipping AI products that actually work
  • Enterprise AI adoption — what it takes to move from pilot to production
  • LLM behavior — understanding why models do what they do

What I build#

When I’m not writing, I’m building:

  • Various AI experiments and tools
