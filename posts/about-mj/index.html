Who is MJ?#

I’m an AI practitioner and agent builder based in New York, with 12+ years of experience driving AI/LLM adoption in large organizations. My focus is on making AI safe, beneficial, and accessible for enterprise customers.

What I Do#

Currently, I work on applied AI at a global real asset investment manager, where I help teams across Finance, Acquisitions, and Client Solutions adopt AI tools. My work spans:

  • Evaluating and integrating AI platforms (Claude, Cohere, Google Agentspace, and others)
  • Building production integrations that connect LLMs to enterprise data securely
  • Translating business requirements into AI architectures
  • Navigating enterprise security, procurement, and stakeholder alignment

I work closely with AI vendors to solve complex integration challenges and establish deployment patterns that work in regulated enterprise environments.

Career Journey#

AI Product & Strategy (2023–Present) Leading AI adoption initiatives, building LLM integrations, RAG pipelines, and AI-powered automation. Hands-on experience with the full stack—from vendor evaluation to production deployment.

Data & Analytics Leadership (2019–2023) Built data warehouses, predictive models, and analytics platforms. Delivered product roadmaps and communicated technical concepts to C-suite stakeholders.

Revenue Management & Analytics (2013–2018) Started in hospitality analytics at Starwood Hotels (now Marriott) and healthcare real estate, building pricing strategies and KPI dashboards.

Education#

  • Cornell University – Master of Management, Nolan School of Hotel Administration
  • Harrisburg University – MS in Analytics (Machine Learning, Time Series, Risk Modeling)

Technical Skills#

  • AI/LLM: Claude, GPT, Gemini, RAG architectures, MCP, prompt engineering
  • Enterprise Integration: OAuth, SSO, API gateways, Kubernetes
  • Data & Cloud: Snowflake, Python, SQL, Azure, AWS, dbt

Beyond Work#

I write about enterprise AI implementation patterns on this blog, contribute to open source AI tooling, and occasionally build iOS apps. I’m passionate about context engineering, agentic AI, and the gap between AI hype and real-world adoption.

Feel free to connect on LinkedIn or explore my writing here.
